# Bounded intelligence acquisition

Experimental snapshot selections support explicitly supplied local bytes or one
immutable official CISA KEV mirror revision. Acquisition retains observed receiver
times, bounded original/partial bytes, immutable terminal records and honest batch
outcomes. A failed or incomplete acquisition yields no usable snapshot.

Portable replay requires the original raw owning manifest, exact byte identities
and consistent receiver/importer provenance. Existing verified enrichment owns
finding application and triage. HTTPS transport and hashes do not authenticate
publisher signatures, establish freshness, infer applicability or grant authority.

ADR 0060 and `docs/INTELLIGENCE_ACQUISITION.md` define this restricted Experimental
contract. Other helpers and direct result construction remain Internal.
