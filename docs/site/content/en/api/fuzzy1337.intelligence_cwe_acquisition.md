# fuzzy1337.intelligence_cwe_acquisition

Experimental explicit local CWE ZIP custody and exact original-byte replay.
Original ZIP, full source/notice declaration, bounded selection and receipt remain
one source-bound family. The effective caller ceiling participates in identity;
pure decoder limits and legacy snapshot families are unchanged.

Publication acknowledgements are independent. Unknown publication never means
rollback or usable successful custody. Reopen preflights original owning artifacts,
strictly reconstructs source/notice/selection/receipt joins and reparses the entire
original ZIP. Catalog days remain distinct from observed acquisition instants.

This SDK-only profile has no network, CLI or model mutation. Source attribution,
notice preservation and byte identity do not establish publisher authentication,
legal rights, notice applicability or target risk.
